AUTO-SYNC AFTER A BUILD INSTALL
================================
When Kodi starts on a fresh build (or any build that does not have
ReAuthorize's setup marker), ReAuthorize automatically restores your
credentials from the cloud and pushes them to every supported add-on
in one step.

No toggles, no configuration, no action required.

Once setup is complete the marker is written, and future starts are
instant - no sync is run until you install another new build.

A new add-on, or one you open for the first time, is noticed within
a few minutes (or at the next Kodi start) and gets your sign-ins by
itself. To do it by hand, go to the  Backup & Device  tab and press
"Save & Backup Now" - this backs up your credentials and pushes them
to all add-ons in one step.

For a targeted re-push without a backup, go to the  Manage  tab and
press "Re-Authorize My Services," or use the individual "Sync Add-ons"
button in each service's tab.


Getting Help
============

If something is not working, here are a few things to check first:

- Make sure you completed authorization (the service row shows your username).
- If a sync failed, try the manual Re-Sync button for that service.
- If credentials were lost after a reinstall, try Settings > Backup & Device > Restore from Vault.

Need more help?
---------------

Come find us on Telegram - the link is in the addon's forum/info page.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT post about ReAuthorize issues on the official Kodi forums.
ReAuthorize is a third-party addon and is outside the scope of Kodi's official
support. Posting there about it may get you warned or removed.

Bring your question to Telegram instead - describe what happened and
what you expected, and someone will help you out.

Local troubleshooting
---------------------

1. Turn on Settings > System > Logging > Enable debug logging.
2. Repeat the action that failed.
3. Inspect the local kodi.log for ReAuthorize errors.
4. Turn debug logging off when finished.

The embedded version has no log-upload feature.

HOW TO USE REAUTHORIZE
======================
A plain-English guide for getting set up - no technical knowledge needed.


----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS REAUTHORIZE?
----------------------------------------------------------------
ReAuthorize is the one place in Kodi where you link your streaming
accounts - debrid services, Trakt, Simkl, and others.

Once you link them here, ReAuthorize pushes your credentials to
every supported add-on automatically. You link once, and every
add-on in your build benefits.

The best part: after you install a new build, ReAuthorize restores
everything on its own, at Kodi start. You do not have to open it,
press anything, or even remember it is there. Your credentials are
kept in a personal vault that a build install cannot wipe.

You only need to open ReAuthorize when you want to link a new
service, or re-link one that has lapsed - for example if your
debrid subscription ran out and you renewed it.


----------------------------------------------------------------
ANDROID USERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
There is nothing for you to do. ReAuthorize works on Fire TV,
Shield, Onn, Google TV, phones and tablets with no setup.

You do NOT need an SD card, a USB drive, or external storage of
any kind. An earlier version of this guide said that adding one
would help. That was wrong, and we are sorry if it sent anyone
shopping - it could never have made any difference.

Your backup is kept outside Kodi's own settings, which is what
lets it survive a new build. ReAuthorize picks that spot itself:
it writes a test file and reads it back first, so it only trusts
a place your device really allows. If the best spot is blocked,
it moves down to the next one automatically.

To see exactly where your backup is saved on your device:
  About tab  ->  Storage Report


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - GETTING STARTED
----------------------------------------------------------------
ReAuthorize opens straight to Settings when you launch it.
Work through the service tabs in this menu to authorize each
service you have an account with. Your profile is created
and backed up automatically as you go.

Already signed in to a service inside one of your add-ons?
On this first tab, press:

  "Already Signed In Inside Your Add-ons? Bring Those In"

ReAuthorize will look through your installed add-ons, show you
what it found, and ask before taking anything. Anything you
accept is then shared with your other add-ons and backed up.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - LINK YOUR DEBRID SERVICE (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A debrid service (Real-Debrid, Premiumize, AllDebrid, TorBox,
Easy Debrid, or OffCloud) lets add-ons play content faster and
more reliably. You pay the debrid company directly - it is a
separate subscription from Kodi.

To link a debrid service:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Debrid Services  tab
  2. Find your service and press "Authorize"
  3. Follow the steps shown on screen
  4. When done, your username will appear alongside Sync and
     Revoke options

If you do not have a debrid service, skip this step. Your
add-ons will still work - debrid is optional.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - LINK TRAKT FOR KODI (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Trakt is a free service that tracks what you have watched so
Kodi and compatible add-ons know what is new and what you have
already seen.

To link Trakt:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab
  2. Press "Authorize Trakt for Kodi"
  3. An 8-character code will appear on screen
  4. On any phone, tablet, or computer, go to:

         trakt.tv/activate

  5. Sign in to Trakt (or create a free account)
  6. Enter the code shown on your Kodi screen
  7. Kodi detects the connection automatically - wait a moment
  8. A confirmation message appears when done

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
  As soon as you finish signing in, ReAuthorize sends Trakt to
  every add-on you have installed that uses it. There is nothing
  to choose. Kodi then asks you to restart once so they all pick
  it up.

  ReAuthorize renews the Trakt login by itself, about a month
  before it runs out, and sends the new one to all of your
  add-ons and to your backups. That keeps them working together,
  and keeps them showing up in Trakt as one connected app. (Two
  add-ons renewing the same login at the same time is what used
  to sign people out of all of them.)

  If you install another add-on later, press "Sync Add-ons" on
  the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab to send Trakt to it.

  You can come back and change your choice at any time.

GOOD TO KNOW:
  You are signing in with YOUR Trakt account. ReAuthorize is
  simply the application doing the asking - which is why Trakt
  shows one connected app no matter how many add-ons you use it
  with. That matters, because a free Trakt account is allowed
  one connected app.

NOTE: Trakt requires a Kodi restart before it becomes fully
active. If prompted to restart, do so.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - LINK SIMKL (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Simkl tracks your viewing history across devices, similar to
Trakt.

To link Simkl:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab
  2. Press "Authorize Simkl"
  3. A popup shows a PIN code and the address:

         simkl.com/pin

  4. Go to that address on any device and sign in
  5. Enter the PIN code shown in the Kodi popup
  6. Click Allow when asked for permission
  7. The page may appear to stop loading - that is normal.
     Kodi is checking in the background and will detect
     approval automatically.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - LINK LAST.FM (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Last.fm is a music tracking service. If you use a music add-on
that supports Last.fm, linking it here means it will record
what you listen to automatically.

To link Last.fm:
  1. Install the "Last.fm Scrobbler" add-on from Kodi's add-on repo
  2. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab
  3. Scroll down to the Last.fm section and press "Authorize Last.fm"
  4. Enter your Last.fm username, then your password
  5. A confirmation message appears when the Scrobbler has signed in

No Last.fm API key is needed.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 - YOUTUBE FOR KODI (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
YouTube for Kodi needs its own set of Google API keys. The
add-on ships with shared ones, but they are used by everybody
and run out of daily allowance - that is what causes the
"quota exceeded" errors. Creating your own set fixes it.

There are three values, all made in one free Google account:

  API Key         for ordinary searching and browsing
  Client ID       identifies the app to your Google account
  Client Secret   the password that goes with the Client ID

When you create the Client ID, you MUST choose the application
type "TVs and Limited Input devices". That is the type that
lets you sign in with a code on screen. Picking any other type
will not work, because Kodi has no web browser to return to.

To store them here:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Streaming Services  tab
  2. Under "Step 1", enter the API Key, Client ID and Client Secret
  3. Press "Sync Keys to YouTube for Kodi"
  4. Under "Step 2", press "Sign in to YouTube"

ReAuthorize keeps these keys and puts them back after a build
install, so you only ever create them once.

SIGNING IN TO YOUR YOUTUBE ACCOUNT IS SEPARATE:
  The keys above only let the add-on talk to YouTube. To see
  your own subscriptions, watch history, saved playlists and
    Watch Later, you also sign in to YouTube. You do NOT need to
  sign in just to watch or search.

  It is the same sign-in you would do for YouTube on any TV:
  Kodi shows a short code, you enter it at youtube.com/activate
  on your phone or computer, and sign in there with the account
  you already use for YouTube. ReAuthorize never sees your
  password. It only keeps a copy of the sign-in YouTube hands
  back, so a new build does not make you do it again.

  The "Sign in to YouTube" button under Step 2 starts it, and
  Step 2 shows "Signed in" once it has worked.

  YouTube for Kodi will ask you to sign in more than once in a
  row (usually 3 codes) - it warns you about this before it
  starts. That is normal: it links several parts of your
  account, each with its own code. The FIRST code goes in at
  youtube.com/activate, the next ones at google.com/device.
  Wait for each new code to show in Kodi before typing it.

  IMPORTANT: work through every prompt it gives you, and approve
  each one. If you decline any of them, the whole sign-in is
  thrown away - including the parts you already approved - and
  YouTube ends up looking signed out.

  Once you are through it, you are done with it for good.
  ReAuthorize keeps a copy of that sign-in along with your keys,
  so after a build install it comes back on its own and you do
  not sit through those prompts again.

TORRENTCLAW (OPTIONAL, SAME TAB):
  Cosmos can search TorrentClaw, and needs your own free key for
  it. TorrentClaw has no sign-in with a code, so you type the key
  once:
  1. On your phone or computer, sign in at torrentclaw.com, open
     your profile and the API Keys tab, and copy the key
  2. Press "Enter TorrentClaw Key" and type it in
  ReAuthorize checks it with TorrentClaw, gives it to Cosmos, and
  keeps it with your other sign-ins, so you never type it again.
  Cosmos also needs a debrid account to play what it finds.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 - METADATA SERVICES (OPTIONAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Metadata services supply the movie posters, descriptions, cast
info, and artwork shown in Kodi. Most users do not need to do
anything here - the defaults work fine.

If an add-on asks you to enter a Fanart.TV API key:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Metadata  tab
  2. Enter the API key for the relevant provider
  3. Press "Authorize" where shown

MDBList is on the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab: press "Authorize MDBList",
enter the code shown, and add your API key when it asks.

The TMDB connection is handled automatically by each add-on -
you do not need to set it up here unless specifically asked.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 - ADDING OR REMOVING A SERVICE LATER
----------------------------------------------------------------
You can add or remove any service at any time.

  To add a service:
    Go to that service's tab and press Authorize.

  To remove a service:
    Open the  Manage  tab and press "Remove a Service."
    Choose a category, then the service you want to remove.
    Confirm the removal - your backup is updated automatically.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9 - YOU ARE DONE!
----------------------------------------------------------------
There is nothing to press. Each time you link a service,
ReAuthorize does three things by itself:
  - Saves your credentials to the local vault
  - Backs them up to the server
  - Sends them to every supported add-on in your build

You will see "Saved and backed up." when it has done so.

An add-on you install later, or open for the first time later,
gets your sign-ins by itself within a few minutes or at the next
Kodi start. You do not need to touch settings inside each one.

"Save & Backup Now" on the  Backup & Device  tab is still there
if you ever want to do it by hand.

NOTE: Some services (especially Trakt) require a Kodi restart
before they become fully active.


----------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER INSTALLING A NEW BUILD
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is where ReAuthorize really shines.

When you install a new build, ReAuthorize automatically restores
your credentials and pushes them to all your add-ons, at Kodi
start. You do not need to open it or do anything at all.

If a service is not authorized after installing a new build:
  Open ReAuthorize, go to the  Backup & Device  tab, and press
  "Save & Backup Now" - this also pushes credentials to all add-ons.

If it is still not working, try the  Manage  tab and press
"Re-Authorize My Services" to force a fresh credential push.

If that does not fix it, check the supported add-ons list in
the  About  tab. If your add-on is not listed, contact us:

         tinyurl.com/ReAuthHelp

  It will be added within 24 hours.

You can also go to  Backup & Device  to restore credentials
from a cloud backup if the automatic restore did not find them.


----------------------------------------------------------------
FOR BUILD CREATORS - BEFORE YOU ZIP YOUR BUILD
----------------------------------------------------------------
ReAuthorize stores your personal credentials in Kodi settings.
If you have authorized services on the install you are
packaging, those credentials will be in the build zip unless
you clear them first.

The order matters. Prepare This Build for Distribution is the
LAST thing you do inside Kodi:
  1. Do any clean-up you do inside Kodi first (a wizard's cache,
     thumbnails, packages and so on)
  2. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Reset & Clear  tab
  3. Press the green "START - Prepare This Build for Distribution"
     at the bottom of the tab
  4. Press "Remove and close"
  5. Choose "Truncate too" to also truncate the database tables
     (watched history, TV guide, music library, view settings), as a
     wizard's Truncate Tables does. Your add-ons, their settings,
     Kodi's add-on database and the thumbnails are kept. Both need
     Kodi to close straight after, which is why one button does both.
     Choose "Sign-ins only" if you already truncated the tables.
  6. A progress window shows each step (about a minute). It ends
     with "Tables and sign-ins are clean. Kodi will now force close."
     Please let it finish: Kodi force closes by itself when done,
     with every sign-in removed
  7. Do any clean-up you do OUTSIDE Kodi now (.pyc files and so on),
     with Kodi still closed
  8. Zip the build folder RIGHT AWAY - before re-opening Kodi

Do NOT delete ReAuthorize's addon_data folder, anything in
userdata/Database, or rows in Kodi's add-on database (Addons33.db)
by hand. Prepare This Build for Distribution already removes
everything that matters, and hand deleting add-on data is how
builds end up with add-ons that will not open.

IMPORTANT: If you re-open Kodi before zipping, your credentials
reload automatically. You will need to run "Prepare This Build
for Distribution" again before zipping.

Users who install your build will not see your credentials.
When they first open ReAuthorize, the Read Me First tab will
guide them through getting started. They can then use the
service tabs to authorize their own accounts.


----------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING DOES NOT LOOK RIGHT
----------------------------------------------------------------
If an add-on is still not authorized after a new build:
  1. Open ReAuthorize and go to the  Manage  tab
  2. Press "Re-Authorize My Services"
  3. If Trakt is involved, restart Kodi after syncing -
     Trakt requires a restart before changes take effect

If Trakt is missing from one particular add-on, press
"Sync Add-ons" on the  Trakt & Scrobble  tab and restart Kodi.
If it is still missing, check the supported add-ons list in
the  About  tab.

To check what is connected across your whole build, go to
the  Manage  tab and press "View Status."

If Re-Authorize My Services does not fix it, check the supported
add-ons list in the  About  tab to confirm your add-on is
listed. If it is not listed, contact us:

         tinyurl.com/ReAuthHelp

It will be added within 24 hours.
